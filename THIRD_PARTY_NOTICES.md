# Third-party software and attribution

This local review snapshot builds on LangGraph and LangChain for model and graph execution, and established scientific libraries for statistics, tabular data and spatial analysis. Dependency versions are recorded in [pyproject.toml](pyproject.toml) and [uv.lock](uv.lock). Their respective upstream license terms continue to apply.

The retained local `scientific_research_agent` package supplies source adapters, registries, numerical tools and compatibility workflow code. It is local project material reused by the later agents, not a claim of a newly invented numerical library. Its original independent license and authorship have not been established by the available source checkout. The [provenance document](docs/provenance.md) records that boundary.

No license has been selected on the owner's behalf. This snapshot is prepared for local review; confirm project authorship, third-party reuse rights and the appropriate repository license before public redistribution. No paper PDFs, external datasets or provider credentials are distributed here.
